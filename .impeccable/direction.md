# Direction Contract — Quiet Commerce Console

## World

ShopEasy uses the restraint of a focused desktop tool: near-black canvas, graphite separators, compact system typography, real product imagery, and a single muted green reserved for available actions and positive state. The visual reference is Cursor-like density and clarity without copying Cursor branding or editor-specific ornament.

## First Viewport

- A 56px utility navigation exposes the three primary destinations, account actions, cart, wishlist, and the project attribution.
- The main statement, “Everyday goods, edited down.”, sits beside a real featured product rather than above promotional metrics.
- Search and category navigation are available before the catalog grid.
- Product imagery carries the color; interface chrome remains neutral.

## Visitor Path

Search or choose a category, inspect a featured product, browse compact catalog entries, then add to the bag or open product details. Wishlist, account, cart, and order history remain accessible from global navigation.

## Signature Interaction

Product imagery receives one restrained scale shift on card/workbench hover. The rest of the interface communicates state through borders, text, and the action green rather than distributed animation.

## Cross-Surface Reach

The same canvas, surface, border, type, radius, focus, control, and state tokens govern the catalog, detail, cart, checkout, authentication, profile, wishlist, orders, empty states, toasts, and footer.

## Honest Risk

The dark, dense treatment is less conventionally “retail” and depends on strong product photography for warmth. Responsive spacing and high-contrast focus states keep it from becoming austere or difficult to scan.

## Quality Bar

- No gradients, decorative blur, floating ornaments, fabricated proof, or oversized marketing statistics.
- Borders do the structural work; shadows are used only where transient UI needs separation.
- Buttons name concrete actions and show visible keyboard focus.
- Layout remains complete at 1440px and 390px.
